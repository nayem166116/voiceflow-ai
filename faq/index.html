FAQ / answers

Clear answers before the first call.

VoiceFlow AI is designed for focused workflows, measurable outcomes, and responsible handoffs.

What does VoiceFlow AI automate?

It can handle structured phone conversations such as appointment booking, common support questions, lead qualification, order tracking, and routing. Each flow is defined by the work it needs to complete.

Can a person take over a call?

Yes. Handoff rules are part of the call brief. A team can define which topics, phrases, or situations should move to a human.

Do I need a large support team?

No. A focused call flow is useful for a small team as long as the goal and boundaries are clear.

How should we start?

Choose one repeatable call type, describe the outcome, write the human handoff rule, and review early conversations before expanding.

Can VoiceFlow AI connect to our tools?

Growth and Enterprise plans are designed for calendar, CRM, helpdesk, telephony, and webhook connections. The exact setup depends on the workflow.
